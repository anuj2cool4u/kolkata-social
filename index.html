Our Story

Heritage on
a plate.

Kolkata Social honours heritage while exploring the depth and complexity of Bengali flavours in new ways.

Led by Chef Ahana Dutt — born and raised in Kolkata, shaped by experience in Sydney's most respected kitchens including Firedoor and Raja — the menu champions local producers and seasonal ingredients, brought to life through traditional techniques and time-honoured family recipes.

More than a restaurant, Kolkata Social is a space for training future talent — fostering a nearly all-female team and championing the development of women in hospitality.

Come for the food. Stay for the stories.
