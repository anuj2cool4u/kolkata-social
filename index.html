Heritage
on a plate.
Third culture
on the palate.

Kolkata Social honours Bengali heritage while exploring the depth and complexity of its flavours in new ways — through a third culture lens.

Led by Chef Ahana Dutt, born and raised in Kolkata and shaped by kitchens including Firedoor and Raja, the menu champions local producers and seasonal ingredients brought to life through traditional techniques and time-honoured family recipes.

More than a restaurant — a space for training future talent, fostering a nearly all-female team, and championing women in hospitality.

Come for the food. Stay for the stories.
