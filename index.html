Our Story

Heritage on a plate.
Through a third culture lens.

Kolkata Social honours Bengali heritage while exploring the depth and complexity of its flavours in new ways.

Led by Chef Ahana Dutt — born and raised in Kolkata, shaped by experience in Sydney's most respected kitchens including Firedoor and Raja — the menu champions local producers and seasonal ingredients, brought to life through traditional techniques and time-honoured family recipes.

More than a restaurant, it is a space for training future talent — fostering a nearly all-female team and championing the development of women in hospitality.
